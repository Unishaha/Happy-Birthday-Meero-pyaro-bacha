💌 A Letter for My Pudku

My sweetest Pudku, 💗

Happy Birthday to the most wonderful person! Today is your special day, and I hope you feel surrounded by love, happiness, laughter and all the beautiful things you deserve. I made this little corner of the internet just for you, because an ordinary birthday wish didn't feel like enough.

You make ordinary moments feel special. Your smile can brighten a difficult day, your laughter can make everything feel lighter, and your presence makes the world feel a little warmer. I hope you never forget how precious you are.

May this new chapter bring you beautiful memories, exciting adventures, peaceful days, wonderful opportunities and countless reasons to smile. May every dream you hold close find its way toward becoming real. May you always have people around you who respect you, support you and remind you how amazing you are.

I hope you keep being your lovely self: curious, funny, caring, silly and wonderfully unique. Never feel that you need to be perfect. You deserve kindness on your good days and your difficult days, too.

Thank you for being you, Pudku. Thank you for the little moments, the laughter, the memories and all the happiness you bring. If I could wrap up every good thing in the world and give it to you, I would. For now, I hope this little website makes you smile.

So make a wish, enjoy your cake, take lots of pictures, laugh as much as you can and make this birthday unforgettable. You deserve a day as lovely as your heart. 🌷

Happy Birthday, my Bachu! 💗🎂

With lots and lots of love, always. 💞
